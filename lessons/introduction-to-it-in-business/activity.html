Business IT Tools Mapping Activity

Choose suitable IT tools for common business and accounting tasks.

Scenario

A small accounting practice wants to improve how it prepares reports, communicates with clients, stores documents, tracks income and expenses, and protects access to financial records.

Instructions

  1. Create a table with these columns: Business task, Recommended tool, Reason, and Security or control.
  2. Add one row for each task: prepare a monthly expense analysis, issue client invoices, hold a remote client meeting, store working papers for team access, and protect employee sign-ins.
  3. Choose tools from the lesson. You may use Excel, QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Drive, OneDrive, firewalls, or multi-factor authentication.
  4. Explain in one or two sentences why each tool matches the task.
  5. Add one appropriate control for each row, such as restricted access, multi-factor authentication, regular backups, or review of automated calculations.

Expected output

Submit one completed five-row table and a short paragraph explaining how the selected tools improve accuracy, efficiency, communication, or information security.

Reflection

Which selected tool would have the greatest effect on the accounting practice, and why?
