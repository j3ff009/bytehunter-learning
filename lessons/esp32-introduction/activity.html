Plan a Safe LED Circuit

Translate the blink program into a checked wiring and testing plan.

Materials

  • ESP32 development board
  • Solderless breadboard and jumper wires
  • One LED and a suitable current-limiting resistor
  • Data-capable USB cable

Before power

  1. Locate a safe output-capable GPIO using the exact board pinout.
  2. Draw the circuit path from GPIO through resistor and LED to ground.
  3. Label LED anode and cathode.
  4. Ask a partner or instructor to check the plan.
Stop and inspect: Disconnect USB power before changing wires. Never connect a GPIO directly across power and ground.

Program reasoning

Annotate each line of the blink program. Explain what runs once, what repeats, what HIGH and LOW do, and why the delay is visible to a person.

Reflection

If the LED does not blink, write a safe troubleshooting order covering the cable, board and port selection, pin number, LED polarity, resistor, ground, and upload messages.
